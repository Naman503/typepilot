First public release of TypePilot for Android. Download **TypePilot.apk** below, then follow the [install steps](https://github.com/Naman503/typepilot#-install-in-5-steps).

### Added
- **Suggestions on top of every app** with your own keyboard: memory answers the moment a field opens, AI continuations as you type, and reply starters in chats. They stream in word by word.
- **Knowledge memory.** Every form field you fill is remembered as question → answer. Add a PDF, Word or text file (your resume, for example) and it's turned into facts.
- **More than one likely answer.** Up to three answers per field, each on its own row with a coloured % pill. Tap the right one.
- **Memory + AI side by side.** Real questions ("Why do you want to join us?") get a full AI-written answer under your saved answers.
- **App-aware memory.** What you type in an app is preferred in that app; optionally, keep it only there.
- **Floating assistant.** ✦ opens Fix grammar, Grammar + flow, Best version, tones, translation (English, Hindi, Hinglish) and Write with AI, without closing the keyboard. Changed words are highlighted.
- **Edge tab.** A translucent ✦ in your Material You colour that slides in and fades. Drag it, or hold it to hide TypePilot in that app.
- **Smart switch for payment apps.** Hidden apps turn accessibility fully off while they're open, and it comes back on afterwards, even after an update, reboot or the phone killing the app.
- **Backup and restore** of memory, phrases, stats and settings (API keys are never included).
- **Models:** Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama (found on your Wi-Fi), and 18 on-device models rated for your phone, with a benchmark.
- **Insights:** acceptance, words and time saved, live CPU and memory, and latency for every stage.
- **TypePilot Keyboard** for phones where accessibility apps are blocked.

### Fixed
- A field could show the answer meant for the field below it (the next field's label was picked as its question).
- Suggestions are only looked up while the keyboard is open, so a field focused by an app with the keyboard closed costs nothing.
- The AI answer to a form question was cut off after 8 words.
- The floating assistant could be clipped on short screens; it now moves to the top when there's no room beside the field.

### Notes
- If you were running a build you made yourself, back up your memory first (Settings → Apps, memory & privacy → Back up), uninstall it once, install this release and restore. The release is signed with a different key.
- arm64 phones only (almost every phone from the last six years).
